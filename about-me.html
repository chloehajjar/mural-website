HOW IT ALL STARTED: My passion for graffiti art started back in 2024 during my first year of GBDA studies. For my final project in GBDA 101, I decided to create a magazine showcasing some of the most beautiful artworks of my region.

With a camera in my hands, I wandered the colorful alleyways of Downtown Kitchener, capturing true masterpieces with a simple click of a button. All the photos showcased here are the ones I took during this great adventure!
